Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Oct. 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy for SimplyTee ("we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

  • Visit our website at https://www.simplytee.com or any website of ours that links to this Privacy Policy
  • Use SimplyTee, a store for simple and comfortable t-shirts
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including any marketing or events

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Policy will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. We are responsible for making decisions about how your personal information is processed. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at simplytee.mgmt@gmail.com.

1. What information we collect

We only collect information you give us when you place an order or contact us. This includes your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address, plus the details of your order such as shirt, size, color, and quantity.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, confirm, and deliver your order
  • To reply to your questions, concerns, or suggestions
  • To send you updates about new releases, only if you agree to receive them

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We only share what is needed with the courier delivering your order and the payment service you choose to pay with.

4. Third party services on this website

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and page components from jsDelivr. When your browser loads these, those services may receive your IP address. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed to complete your order and handle any returns or concerns, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to object, to have your data erased or blocked, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To exercise any of these rights, email us at simplytee.mgmt@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
